# BitAim v4.1 — team selection, stable routes and one-shot picker

This update addresses the user's real-game screenshots and feedback. The previous v4.0 build incorrectly treated every detected coin as a possible target. Its picker continued intercepting touches after a selection. It also lacked reliable target persistence and alternate-route validation.

## Resulting behaviour

- Your puck colour is read from the left score badge and compared with the opponent's right badge. Blue and teal are distinguished by their coloured rims, including striped blue skins. If ownership cannot be established, automatic targeting is withheld.
- Both teams remain collision obstacles. Opponent coins cannot be selected as a target or used as an intentional combination intermediate. Unknown shapes are filtered only when the two chromatic team references are distinct; uncertain pieces otherwise remain obstacles. A flat chat bubble is not accepted as a score badge.
- A valid target and route stay selected while the striker is stationary. Small position jitter is suppressed. A materially better alternative requires sustained improvement after repositioning; brief uncertain detections hide guidance rather than immediately switching targets.
- PICK is a one-gesture interaction. Selecting your coin posts the return to pass-through mode after finger-up has been consumed. Wrong-colour taps, empty taps, accidental drags and cancelled gestures also exit the picker. Subsequent striker gestures are intended for the game.
- Tap the floating icon in AUTO to choose another coin. Hold it for about half a second to cycle the available routes for that selected coin. Drag the icon to reposition it. Tap into PICK, then tap the icon again to hide lines.
- Routing searches direct shots, a single striker cushion, one-transfer combinations and two-transfer combinations. Only one route is drawn at a time. Cyan tracks the striker, orange tracks intermediate coins, and green tracks your final target to its pocket. Direct routes are preferred when a validated direct route exists.
- Gold-position searches run on a separate worker and use a copied scene. They do not block drawing. Drawings are thinner than v4.0.

## What the route checks mean

Geometry checks include complete puck radii, extra clearance, conservative cuts, all collision obstacles, pocket approach and rail limits. A nominal continuous-time collision model then rejects routes that fail to reach the chosen pocket, hit an unintended first object, scratch the striker or pot an opponent coin in that model.

The model assumes circular pieces, no spin, restitution 0.96, wall normal restitution 0.90, striker mass ratio 1.2, velocity retention 0.70 per second, an inferred rail position and a pocket radius of 5% of board width. It checks three assumed launch speeds. These values are **not calibrated against the actual game**. A passing model is a conditional prediction, not a guaranteed real-game pot or a measured power recommendation. The app cannot provide 100% accuracy from screenshots, especially with hidden pieces, moving scenes, spin and uncertain power.

Search is deliberately bounded to one striker cushion and at most two intermediate coins. It is not an exhaustive arbitrary-depth planner. Pocket/rail estimates and colour-badge visibility remain limits.
